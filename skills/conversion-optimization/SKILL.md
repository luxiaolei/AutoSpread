# Conversion optimization

Reproduce the target flow before proposing changes. Combine observed friction,
analytics, and user evidence; do not optimize a step whose denominator is
unknown. State the hypothesis, change, primary metric, guardrail, and test
window. Prefer one clear change at a time and verify the implemented state.
