# Customer research

Start from a decision the research must support. Search for first-hand user
language, competitor and alternative evidence, and relevant objections. Record
source, date, confidence, and the difference between a quote and an inference.
Return a small segment map, evidence table, open questions, and implications for
the product or message. Never present a hypothesis as a customer fact.
