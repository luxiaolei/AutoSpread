# Content and video

Use the research and product context as the source of claims. Choose the format
for the audience and distribution channel, then produce a brief, draft, script,
or asset. Mark unsupported claims and placeholders. A generated asset is a
draft until it passes factual review, brand review, and the requested delivery
check.
