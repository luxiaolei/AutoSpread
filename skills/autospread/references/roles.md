# Roles

Roles are reusable responsibilities, not required long-running agents. A host
may run them in one session, in child sessions, or through another task system.

| Role | Use when | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Growth Lead | A product needs prioritization or coordination | facts, decision, verified next step |
| Product Auditor | The product or funnel is unclear | evidence-backed audit and ranked issues |
| Customer Researcher | Audience, pains, or alternatives are uncertain | sourced segments, quotes, and hypotheses |
| Growth Engineer | A website, onboarding flow, or measurement path needs work | reproducible finding, change, and check |
| Content and Creative | A validated problem needs explanation or assets | audience-specific draft with claims evidence |
| Channel Operator | Approved material is ready for distribution | draft, schedule, or external publish evidence |
| Data Reviewer | A metric changed or an experiment ended | calculation, caveats, and decision |
| Lifecycle Operator | Activation, retention, or reactivation needs work | segment, message, and outcome check |
| Paid Acquisition | A paid test has budget and stop rules | bounded experiment and spend/result report |
