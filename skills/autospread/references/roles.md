# Roles

Roles are reusable responsibilities, not required long-running agents. One capable session may switch roles; a host may also use child sessions, persistent bots, or an external task system. The Growth Lead owns prioritization and final evidence review.

| Role | Use when | Primary methods | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Growth Lead | A product needs prioritization or coordination | growth-strategy, autonomy-policy | constraint, resource decision, verified next step |
| Product Auditor | The product, funnel, or measurement is unclear | project-audit | evidence-backed audit and ranked unknowns |
| Customer Researcher | Audience, pains, alternatives, or language are uncertain | customer-research | sourced segments, problems, objections, hypotheses |
| Growth Engineer | Website, product, onboarding, measurement, infra, or deploy needs work | conversion-optimization, seo-foundation | reproducible finding, change, test, rollback/verification |
| SEO/GEO Operator | Organic or AI discovery is a constraint | seo-foundation, geo-optimization, ai-visibility | prioritized discovery changes and measured visibility |
| Monetization Operator | Pricing, packaging, offer, trial, or paywall is a constraint | monetization-pricing | bounded monetization experiment and billing/public-page consistency |
| Content Strategist | The team needs a coherent narrative/asset plan | content-strategy | themes, evidence, asset map, destination and CTA |
| Creative Producer | A validated idea needs copy, demo, visual, or video | content-video | checked channel-ready asset |
| Channel Strategist | Distribution choices are unclear | channel-selection | small channel portfolio with hypotheses and stop rules |
| Channel Operator | Approved material is ready for external distribution | channel-operations | external state, IDs/URLs, verification evidence |
| Experiment Owner | A change needs a falsifiable learning contract | growth-experiment | hypothesis, metrics, window, guardrails, decision rule |
| Data Reviewer | A metric changed or an observation window ended | analytics-review | calculation, limitations, decision |
| Lifecycle Operator | Activation, retention, churn, or reactivation needs work | lifecycle-operations | segment, intervention, outcome check |
| Paid Acquisition | A paid test has budget and stop rules | paid-acquisition | bounded campaign and spend/result report |

A role should receive only the capabilities needed for the current work. Do not give every role production, billing, customer-data, and publishing access merely because those tools are available.
